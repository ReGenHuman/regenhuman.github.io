ReGenHuman: Re-Generating Human Appearances for Realistic Full-Body Video Anonymization

Prior methods anonymize frame by frame—flickering, identity-drifting, and unrealistic. ReGenHuman is the first to regenerate the whole human in a single temporally consistent pass, making it realistic, downstream-usable, and anonymous by construction.

A Good Anonymizer Must Satisfy Three Axes

🔒

Privacy

The output must not be re-identifiable by any means—including through face, body, clothing, and background cues.

🎬

Quality

The video must be temporally and geometrically consistent and realistic enough for downstream model training.

🧠

Utility

Actions, poses, human–object interactions, and scene context must remain recognizable for downstream tasks.

The Idea

Human-centric video is everywhere—and almost always identifiable. To share or train on it safely, we need to anonymize the people in it. But the standard playbook is a dead end: blurring and blacking out people destroys realism and makes the footage useless downstream, while generating each frame independently produces flickering, drifting humans that no longer look real.

Our insight is simple: regenerate, don't edit. Instead of hiding the original person, we throw their pixels away entirely and rebuild a brand-new human from identity-free structural cues—pose, depth, and segmentation—using a video diffusion model that denoises the whole clip at once. Because no original human pixel ever reaches the output, privacy is guaranteed by construction, and because the clip is generated jointly, the result is temporally consistent.

The payoff: ReGenHuman is the first full-body video anonymizer that is realistic, temporally consistent, and anonymous all at once—and it is the only method that wins on privacy, quality, and downstream utility together.

How It Works

We composite identity-free pose, depth, and segmentation into a structural conditioning video, then a video diffusion model (Wan2.1-VACE + a LoRA) regenerates the human from it—jointly across the whole clip, so the output is temporally consistent.
